Tips for a good scan

  • Put one object on a flat, plain surface that looks different from the object (a dark object on a light surface, or the other way).
  • Hold the phone flat, straight above the object. The four parts of the logo show which side of the phone is too high: red = very high, orange = a little high, green = fine. Press the red side down until all four are green.
  • Zoom: use 3× (the chip at the top). The object should fill about half of the screen. Hold the phone about 40 cm above it.
  • Keep all of the object in the picture and avoid hard shadows.
  • Use Tilt to straighten the object and crop close to it.
  • Measure the longest side of the real object with a ruler or caliper and type it. Type the depth (thickness) too.
  • The size is an estimate. Check it with a real sample.
