Tips for a good scan

  • Put one object on a flat, plain surface that looks different from the object (a dark object on a light surface, or the other way).
  • Hold the phone flat, straight above the object. The four parts of the logo show which side of the phone is too high: red = very high, orange = a little high, green = fine. The + slides toward the middle as the phone gets flat. When it fits into the gap of the logo, the logo turns green. Press the red side down until all four are green.
  • Zoom: use 1.5× (the chip at the top). Put the part on an A4 or Letter sheet and hold the phone so the whole sheet fits inside the orange dashed frame (tap its tag to switch A4 / Letter). That is about 1½ sheets high: A4 45 cm (17½ in), Letter 42 cm (16½ in). 35–55 cm (14–22 in) is fine. Tap How to to see it as a short animation.
  • Keep all of the object in the picture and avoid hard shadows.
  • Use Tilt to straighten the object and crop close to it.
  • L is the longest side of the real object: measure it with a ruler or caliper and type it. W, the short side, is filled in from the photo. If your caliper shows something else, type it and the outline is stretched a little to match. D is the depth: how deep the part sinks into the foam (the side view in the drawing).
  • No object at hand? Use Shape (camera screen or Your Library): rectangle, rectangle with round corners or cylinder, with typed sizes.
  • In Your Library tap the orange ×1 + (or the small Both tag) on a card to set how many of that part you have and whether it goes in the Base, the Lid or Both (the app chooses).
  • Paper: put the part on a sheet of paper and show the whole sheet. If the app sees the paper it asks what it is (A4, Letter, A3 or your own size, for example a sticky note next to the part). It flattens the picture and reads the size, you only check it. Hold the phone straight above, not too close.
  • The size is an estimate. Check it with a real sample.
