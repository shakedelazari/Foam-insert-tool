Tips for a good scan

  • Put one object on a flat, plain surface that looks different from the object (dark object on a light surface, or the other way).
  • Hold the phone flat, straight above the object. The cross turns green when the phone is flat.
  • Keep the whole object in the picture, about 40 to 60 cm away. Avoid hard shadows.
  • Use Tilt to straighten the object, and crop close to it.
  • Measure the longest side of the real object with a ruler or caliper and type it. Type the depth (thickness) too.
  • The size is an estimate. Check it with a real sample.
